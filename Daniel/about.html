about →

Danniel VIllamizar is a film director and artist based in Paris. His work combines visual art and storytelling with a distinctive voice. He is known for exploring our relationship with the natural world and creating visually rich films, often shot in challenging environments.

He was named one of ADC Young guns — Best creatives under 30. His work includes award-winning shorts, music videos, feature documentaries, and creative collaborations with global brands like Louis Vuitton, Meta, and Audemars Piguet. In 2022, he became a member of the Directors Guild of America (DGA). He is particularly interested in emerging technologies, immersive art and new mediums that push the boundaries of cinematic storytelling.

In 2025, he directed the film for Cercle Odyssey, a monumental 360° immersive experience combining live music, cinema, and installation art.
